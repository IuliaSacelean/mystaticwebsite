Frequently Asked Questions

📱 The game doesn’t start or crashes

Try the following steps:

  • Restart your device.
  • Ensure you have the latest version of the game installed.
  • Check that your device has enough free storage.
  • Close other apps running in the background.

🃏 My cards or progress disappeared

All game data is stored locally on your device. If progress is missing:

  • Make sure the game wasn’t deleted or reinstalled.
  • Check if your device recently performed a system reset.
  • Verify that storage permissions are enabled.

🔔 I can’t unlock new cards

Ensure you meet the in‑game requirements for unlocking new cards. If the issue persists, contact support using the details below.

💳 Are there any in‑app purchases?

No. Meow Battle Cats – Card Game does not include in‑app purchases.
