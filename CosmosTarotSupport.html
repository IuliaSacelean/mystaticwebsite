Cosmos Tarot Support

Welcome to the Cosmos Tarot support page. Here you’ll find guidance for tarot readings, astrology charts, and answers to common questions.

Getting Started

First steps with Cosmos Tarot.

  • Explore the Tarot Deck: Tap any card to view meanings, upright and reversed interpretations, and cosmic insights.
  • Choose a Spread: Select from classic spreads like 1‑Card, 3‑Card, Celtic Cross, or themed spreads for love, career, and clarity.
  • Generate Your Birth Chart: Enter your date, time, and place of birth to calculate your astrology chart.
  • Read Your Cosmic Summary: After a reading or chart calculation, view a personalized interpretation blending tarot and astrology.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and quick fixes.

  • Cards not loading: Close and reopen the app; ensure your device has enough memory available.
  • Birth chart not generating: Double‑check that your birth time and location are entered correctly.
  • App feels slow: Restart the app or your device to refresh memory.
  • Unexpected behavior: Update to the latest version of Cosmos Tarot from the App Store.

FAQ

  • Do I need internet? No. All tarot readings and astrology calculations run locally on your device.
  • Is my birth data stored? No. Birth chart inputs are used only for the calculation and are not saved or transmitted.
  • Can I save spreads? Yes, depending on your device settings, spreads may be stored locally for quick access.
  • Are the interpretations AI‑generated? No. All card meanings and summaries are handcrafted for clarity and accuracy.
