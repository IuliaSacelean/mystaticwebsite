Cosmos Tarot – Privacy Policy

Overview

Cosmos Tarot respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how your data is protected.

Information We Collect

Cosmos Tarot is designed to provide tarot readings, astrology chart calculations, and cosmic summaries without collecting unnecessary data. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number) is collected.
  • No analytics, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs are used.
  • No data is sent to external servers.
  • Birth chart inputs (date, time, location) are processed locally on your device and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Tarot spreads, settings, and preferences remain stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

The app only uses local device storage to:

  • Save your tarot spread history (if enabled).
  • Store your app preferences and settings.
  • Ensure the app runs smoothly on your device.

No information is transmitted, shared, or sold.

Astrology Chart Calculations

Birth chart calculations require date, time, and location of birth. This information is used only to generate your chart and is not stored, uploaded, or shared.

Data Security

All app data is stored locally on your device. Because Cosmos Tarot does not use external servers, your information stays private and under your control.

Children’s Privacy

Cosmos Tarot is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may delete the app at any time to remove all locally stored data. Since no cloud storage is used, uninstalling the app permanently removes all saved information.
